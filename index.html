About Us

Titan Roofing & Restoration helps homeowners and businesses across Central Texas protect their properties with dependable roofing and restoration services. Serving communities such as Temple, Waco, Belton, Killeen, and Salado, the team handles everything from roof repairs to complete roof replacements.

Beyond roofing, the company provides storm and water damage recovery, mold remediation, fire restoration, and interior repairs. Customers can receive professional inspections, written estimates, and documented damage reports to better understand their property's needs.

Property damage can be stressful, so Titan Roofing & Restoration also coordinates with the insurance claims process to make things easier for property owners. Learn more about their services on the official Titan Roofing & Restoration website. The team is available Monday through Friday from 8 AM to 5 PM and Saturday from 8 AM to 3 PM.
